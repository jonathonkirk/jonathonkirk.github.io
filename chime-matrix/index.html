Faust Chime Matrix — 10 × 10 Button Test

Click the power/play button on the widget below, then tap any of the 100 buttons in the grid. Each one rings out on its own for about 10 seconds, like tapping a metallic chime — you don't need to hold the button down. Try tapping several in a row to hear them layer.

Using an iPhone/iPad? Make sure the physical mute (Ring/Silent) switch on the side of the device is off — iOS silences web page audio by default when that switch is flipped, even though apps like YouTube or Music ignore it.
