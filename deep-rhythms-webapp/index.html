Deep Rhythms

A deep rhythm is an onset pattern where every duration that actually occurs between pairs of onsets — measured as the shorter distance around the pulse cycle — occurs a different number of times. Choose an onset count and a pulse count below to find every deep rhythm that exists for that combination, pick which onset anchors pulse 0, and play it back.

After G. Toussaint, The Geometry of Musical Rhythm, Ch. 25, “Deep Rhythms.” The perfectly evenly-spaced (isochronous) pattern for a given onset/pulse count also satisfies the deep condition trivially — it's reported separately below rather than mixed into the results, since it's normally treated as its own category (a regular rhythm).
