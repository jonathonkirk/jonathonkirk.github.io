MUSI 285 Intro to Music Technology: Harmonic Series Explorer [Jonathon Kirk]

Set a fundamental frequency, then bring in harmonics 2–8 (integer multiples of the fundamental) to hear how they combine. The defaults follow 1/n, the natural harmonic falloff of a sawtooth wave — try muting the even-numbered harmonics to hear it move toward a square-wave-like tone. The read-only bars show each harmonic's live frequency in Hz. Built with Faust.
